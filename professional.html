Selected Project 1: AIR+ Core Banking Migration

Context and objective. The AIR+ project involved migration from a legacy Core Banking environment (Orbit) to a target platform (Amplitude). A central challenge in this type of migration is ensuring that data is sufficiently accurate, complete, consistent, and mapped correctly before loading.

My role and contributions. I served as Data Migration Coordinator. My responsibilities included profiling and cleansing legacy data, supporting data mapping between legacy and target structures, and developing or overseeing ETL scripts and transformation activities. I also participated in validation activities intended to protect data integrity before loading.

Technical skills demonstrated. Data profiling, data cleansing, mapping, ETL coordination, validation, database integrity awareness, communication, and coordination across technical and business stakeholders.

Ethical and spiritual integration. Integrity required accurate reporting of data-quality findings rather than hiding exceptions. Stewardship required careful handling of customer and financial information. Service required remembering that accurate core banking records affect customers, employees, and business operations. My guiding principles of Integrity and Service and Continuous Learning and Growth are directly relevant.

Reflection and lesson learned. This project reinforced that a migration is not only a technical transfer. It is a responsibility to preserve meaning and accuracy as information moves between systems. I learned the importance of clear ownership, documented mapping rules, reconciliation, and escalation when data does not meet agreed standards.
